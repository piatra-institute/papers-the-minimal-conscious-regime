# Editorial record

Status: full read and revision complete (2026-10-02).

## Contribution and reader

For consciousness scientists, anaesthesiologists and computational neuroscientists who design or evaluate measures of conscious state: a four-invariant regime hypothesis (between-region recurrence, perturbational repertoire, endogenous temporal depth, stable macrostructure) made measurable on one model class, a constructive proof that the four are non-redundant, and the statistics that a conjunctive test must pay (margins, label noise, sample size, necessity tests, timing nulls), turned into an experiment. Confidence is that of propositions about stated models, checked numerically, and of exact or Monte Carlo statistical calculations under stated assumptions; no claim that the four invariants are the necessary conditions of consciousness.

## Material worth preserving

From the origin material: the separation of constitutive invariants, controllers and self-related capacities; the four invariants and their names; the three-drug crossover with discovery and confirmation cohorts; the competing models A to G; the six refutation outcomes. Its own diagnosis that estimators, non-redundancy and conjunctive statistics were missing became the paper's three tasks.

- Section 7: limits of the model class, of the estimators and of the statistical assumptions; scope.

## Strongest rival

A single-index account: PCI, or distance from criticality, already separates conscious from unconscious conditions in benchmark populations (Casarotto et al. 2016; Maschke et al. 2024), and a four-way conjunction adds parameters and costs sensitivity. The paper takes this seriously in three places: it quantifies the sensitivity cost (80.6% joint detection from four tests at PCI's 94.7%), it shows within the model that passing Q leaves the other invariants open (64.1% of Q-passing networks fail another) while stating that this share depends on the ensemble, and it makes "a simpler model generalizes as well" an explicit refutation outcome with a test (the McNemar comparison on disagreement epochs). A second rival is that the invariants are artefacts of the linear-Gaussian model; section 7 states that each estimator inherits the bias of the fit and that observation noise and leakage require a state-space model.

## Decisions and remaining checks

- Argument: the four invariants are defined by estimators on one model class; the normalization assigns the spectral radius to T, which gives criticality a formal role as a controller. The timing prediction of the origin material (all invariants fail before loss) is replaced by the conjunction's logic (first failure at loss, last recovery at emergence), and the order-statistic nulls show why a naive even-split test is biased in both directions.
- Structure: results ordered from estimator checks to non-redundancy to controllers to statistics to the experiment; the table placed after the paragraph that interprets it so that it does not split across pages.
- Voice: no em-dashes; signature words reduced (exactly 2, carry 1); spelled-out quantities replaced by numerals; no roadmap paragraph.
- Sources: every factual sentence about the literature was checked against an abstract (sources.md lists which); Hudetz et al. 2015 is a rat study and is cited as such; the PCI cutoff value is not quoted because its abstract does not state it; Cogitate is cited for preregistration, not for a cohort split; misattributions and unresolved items of the origin material are listed in research.md and not used.
- Executed: one recorded run (verification/model.json, PASS, 20 of 20 internal checks); 148 claims bound (104 to the run); refs PASS (76 of 76 cited); voice OK.
